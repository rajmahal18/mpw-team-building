# Phase 9 Reference Sources

Official National Privacy Commission sources used for the engineering baseline:

1. NPC Circular No. 2023-06 — Security of Personal Data in the Government and the Private Sector.
   https://privacy.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/2023-compendium-2.pdf

2. NPC FAQ on Circular 2023-06.
   https://privacy.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/v12-19-2024_FAQ-NPC-Circular-2023-06_NNJ_JDN.pdf

3. NPC Advisory No. 2025-02 — Guidelines on Privacy Engineering in Systems Life Cycle Processes.
   https://privacy.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/NPC_Advisory2025-02.pdf

4. NPC Circular No. 2022-04 — Registration of Personal Data Processing Systems, DPO and related requirements.
   https://privacy.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/Circular-2022-04.pdf

5. NPC Circular No. 16-03 — Personal Data Breach Management.
   https://privacy.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2022/01/sgd-npc-circular-16-03-personal-data-breach-management.pdf

6. NPC breach-reporting guidance / DBNMS information.
   https://privacy.gov.ph/pips-and-pics/breach-reporting/

These references are engineering inputs, not legal advice. Current agency-specific requirements should be confirmed with the designated DPO and appropriate officials before production.
